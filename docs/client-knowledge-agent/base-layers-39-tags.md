- Client: the client the document belongs to. If the name typed on the form differs, the upload stops. (Admin at upload)
- Document type: session form, pillar document, review or delivery framework. (Admin at upload)
- Uploaded by: the signed-in admin, shown in the upload log. (System)
- Upload date: the time of upload, shown in the upload log. (System)
- Status: Current, Superseded or Archived. Archived when a client leaves, and restorable if they return. (System)
- Pillar: 1 to 10 or Review, applied per section when a document covers several pillars. (Form: Pillar field)
- Session date: the date of the session, which decides which position is latest. (Form: Date field)
- Session name: the session title, such as "Positioning, Differentiation & Category Framing". (Form: Session field)
- Attendees: names and job titles of those present. (Form: Attendees field)
- BeDeveloped lead: any attendee who is BeDeveloped staff, so "the call with Luke" can be answered. (Form: Attendees field)
- Captured by: the person who completed the form. (Form: Captured by field)
- Session summary: what the session covered and its main outcome. (Form: section 1)
- Discussion topic: each topic discussed. (Form: section 2, Topic column)
- Key point: what was discussed under each topic. (Form: section 2, Key points column)
- Client view: an opinion or position the client stated. (Form: section 2)
- Challenge raised: an objection, risk or concern raised. (Form: section 2)
- Topic area: the area a recommendation sits in, matched to the topic list. Other rows are matched by AI. (Form: section 3, Area column)
- Recommendation: the solution or recommendation given. (Form: section 3)
- Agreed status: whether the recommendation was agreed. (Form: section 3, Agreed? column)
- Action: what needs to happen. (Form: section 4, Action column)
- Action owner: who owns the action. (Form: section 4, Owner column)
- Action due date: when the action is due. (Form: section 4, Due date column)
- Next session date: when the next session takes place. (Form: section 5, Date row)
- Next session focus: what the next session will cover. (Form: section 5, Focus row)
- Preparation needed: what the client or BeDeveloped needs to prepare. (Form: section 5, Preparation needed row)
- Job titles and buyer roles: any role named as a target or decision maker, such as the job titles agreed for an ICP. (AI, from the text)
- Target organisations: organisations, sectors or segments named as targets for outreach. (AI, from the text)
- Competitors: competitors or alternatives named. (AI, from the text)
- Position change: wording that shows a new position replacing an old one, such as an updated value proposition. (AI, from the text)
- Proof points: figures, outcomes, case studies or references. (AI, from the text)
- Associated terms: matches against George's term library, added to help search. (AI, from the text and George's library)
- Main points: the three to five most important points across the whole document. (AI overview)
- Key takeaways: what the client should remember or do differently as a result. (AI overview)
- Common themes: ideas that recur across different topics in the document. (AI overview)
- Key decisions: every decision made, wherever it appears in the document. (AI overview)
- Pillar key points: the main points for that pillar, such as "Value Proposition key points". (AI overview)
- Topic area key points: the main points for one topic area, such as "ICP key points". (AI overview)
- Open questions: anything left unresolved or still to be decided. (AI overview)
- Client context: background on the client's business, market or situation that frames the advice. (AI overview)
